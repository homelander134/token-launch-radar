Methodology — model formula

Implied value = TVL × multiple × growth × maturity × chain breadth.

  • Multiple — a shrinkage blend of the category median multiple with the protocol-family median (weight 3 on the family side): thin categories lean on their family instead of a handful of peers. When neither exists, the market-wide median is used.
  • Peers — each peer's market-cap-per-TVL is winsorized at 5× before any median is taken, so one thin-TVL peer cannot dominate a category.
  • Growth — 1 plus half the 7-day TVL change, with the change clamped to −40% / +60%.
  • Maturity — 0.8 to 1.0 from protocol age, rising to 1.0 at 3 years; unknown age counts as 1 year.
  • Chain breadth — +2% per extra chain, up to 5 extra chains.
  • Confidence — 0.5 × min(peers used ÷ 15, 1) + 0.25 for known age + 0.25 for known 7-day change (0.4 each when unknown). It scores how much evidence the multiple rests on, not how good the protocol is.
